The Dark Knights kicked off the season with a 5-3 win over the Crows at Sherwood, in a game that had a little bit of everything — new faces in new spots, an eight-month comeback, and, in honor of Dolly Parton's passing that day, a faceoff soundtrack nobody was expecting.
 
## A New-Look Lineup
 
The lineup looked a little different to start the year. Jay played wing for the first time, sliding Rachel back to D in his place. Jeffrey and Dawne swapped spots too, with Jeffrey taking his first shift at center and Dawne moving out to wing. Brian was also back on a wing after tearing his meniscus and taking eight months to recover — and he marked the return with a goal, which is about as good a way to end that layoff as there is.
 
Jeffrey's center debut came with a highlight of its own: he blocked a shot with his cup and got up like it was nothing.
 
## Tied Up, Then Pulling Away
 
The Dark Knights did what the Dark Knights do, building a 2-1 lead before letting the Crows claw it back to 3-3. And a surprise to no one, Dawne was the first to the box — picking up the first penalty of the season for holding. The Crows needed about 20 seconds on the power play to make it count. Lacy held things down in net the rest of the way, and the Knights answered back, pulling ahead for good behind a two-goal night from Chris.
 
## Empty Net, Final Buzzer
 
With under two minutes left, the Crows pulled their goalie looking for the equalizer, and Tabitha made them pay, finishing into the empty net for the game-winner and a 5-3 final.

## Three Stars
1. AJ — Making her Bronze debut, AJ slotted in on D like she'd been playing with this group all year.
2. Chris Godzyk — Two goals in the opener.
3. Tabitha Knight — Iced the game with the empty-net winner.
